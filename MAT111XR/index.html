TITLE

GOG_World

AUTHOR(S)

Aidan Lo (me) & Roberto Ortiz (group partner)

ABSTRACT

This project is a 3D world made in Unity that allows the person to explore and interact with a world in VR (designed for Oculus/Meta Quest 3) and in most laptop browsers.

It is centered around a scene of violence, of urban warfare between two sides, RED and BLU, and the struggle an intelligence operative for hire can have to gather crucial resources and information from the enemy. This is just one possible scenario of warfare explored, given 3D virtual form.

The world was designed over 5 weeks with myself and my partner, Roberto Ortiz, on Apple laptops in Unity using Unity Version Control and Meta Quest Building Blocks, plus free assets from online and from the teacher.

The purpose of this world was to explore the process of creating and executing a Unity 3D world with interactive elements for the player in VR (designed for the Oculus / Meta Quest 3 Headset), creating a storyline and world for the player to participate in and explore.

The purpose of the presentation attached was to educate an audience (the classmates and teacher) about the world we designed, its features, environment, experience, and interactions.
