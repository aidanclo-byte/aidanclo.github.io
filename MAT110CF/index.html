What People Miss in the Moment

A conceptual proposal about people's selective and effective attention.

This project was made for MAT110CF. It explores a concept that touches on limited human attention.

Open PDF in a new tab ↗

ABSTRACT

This project is a presentation created to present a concept that touches on human attention and its flaws through capturing what is not caught through video and audio while demonstrating knowledge and understanding of what exactly constitutes a concept in media arts, plus an analytical perspective of a proper concept.

It is centered around a singular hair-pinable device which captures what the user does not pay attention to, then has its files extracted to a separate device for the recordings to be viewed.

The concept was imagined during a late-night shower, and the presentation immediately after using Google Slides and supercraft.ai (for image generation).

The purpose of this presentation was to educate an audience (the class) about a possible concept to be tackled by designers in the future while simultaneously serving as proof of understanding the MAT110CF class’s content.
